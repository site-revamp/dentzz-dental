# Dentzz Dental Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

Dentzz Dental is a premium dental healthcare provider established in 2006, specialising in dental implants, cosmetic dentistry, full-mouth reconstruction and advanced dental treatments. The company operates across Mumbai, New Delhi and Dubai.

The redesigned homepage focuses on presenting Dentzz Dental as a premium, technology-driven dental care provider. It targets patients seeking specialist dental treatments, smile transformations and advanced restorative procedures, including international patients.

The new design adopts a sophisticated black-and-white visual identity, combining editorial typography, structured layouts and clear appointment-focused navigation to create a more distinctive digital experience.

## 2. Main Improvements

### 1. Stronger Homepage Messaging and Conversion

**Previous issue:** The original homepage lacked a sufficiently distinctive premium introduction and a clear presentation of its primary conversion opportunities.

**Implemented improvement:**
- Introduced a more focused hero section with the headline "Art of a Confident Smile".
- Strengthened the premium dental care positioning.
- Added prominent Book an Appointment and Call Dentzz Dental CTAs.
- Introduced a sticky two-button CTA for easier access to appointment enquiries.

### 2. Simplified Service Navigation and Information Architecture

**Previous issue:** Dental services, technologies and supporting information needed a clearer structure to help visitors discover relevant treatments.

**Implemented improvement:**
- Reorganised the homepage around treatments, advanced technology, patient information and clinic locations.
- Introduced a six-icon service illustration system arranged in two rows of three.
- Created dedicated presentation areas for treatments and advanced dental technologies.
- Improved the content hierarchy to make important information easier to scan and understand.

### 3. Stronger Trust Presentation and Patient Journey

**Previous issue:** Important information about clinical expertise, technology, treatments and clinic locations needed a more connected presentation to support patient confidence and enquiries.

**Implemented improvement:**
- Introduced dedicated sections for dental specialists and clinical credentials.
- Improved the presentation of advanced dental technology and treatment information.
- Added a structured patient journey.
- Brought Mumbai, New Delhi and Dubai clinic information into a dedicated location section.
- Consolidated important contact and conversion information within the homepage and footer.

## 3. Design Improvements

### Visual Identity and Typography
- Adopted an approved black-and-white colour scheme with subtle grey tonal variations.
- Introduced Cormorant Garamond for editorial-style headings.
- Used Manrope for body text, navigation and buttons.
- Established a more consistent typography and visual hierarchy throughout the homepage.

### Layout and User Experience
- Introduced an editorial-style hero composition.
- Created clearly differentiated sections for treatments, technology, specialists and locations.
- Used structured spacing and content groupings to improve readability.
- Added six custom SVG service icons for a consistent visual presentation.
- Implemented a responsive mobile navigation menu and accessible sticky conversion actions.

### Overall Website Appearance
The redesigned homepage moves towards a cleaner, more sophisticated presentation that reflects Dentzz Dental's premium positioning. The monochrome visual direction, refined typography, custom graphic elements and organised content structure create a consistent experience while keeping clinical expertise and appointment enquiries central to the design.

## 4. Design Direction

The redesign follows three core principles:

- **Clinical Precision:** Structured layouts, custom dental-related graphic elements and technology-focused presentations.
- **Smile Artistry:** Editorial typography and carefully composed smile-focused visual concepts.
- **Premium Patient Experience:** A restrained monochrome palette, refined photography direction and clear patient-focused navigation.

The overall objective is to balance premium visual presentation with clinical credibility, usability and clear conversion pathways.
